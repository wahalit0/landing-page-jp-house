Welcome to JP House

Your Peaceful Sanctuary in the Heart of Ubud

JP House is strategically located at Jl. Sri Wedari, Ubud, Bali offering a harmonious blend of traditional Balinese warmth and modern convenience. Whether you are traveling solo or with a partner, our homestay provides a quiet escape while keeping you close to Ubud's vibrant art markets, cafes, and sacred sites.

Enjoy spacious rooms equipped with top-tier amenities, high-speed internet for digital nomads, and expansive private balconies where you can unwind amidst tropical breezes.
